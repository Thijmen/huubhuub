The phone app now looks like its design. Conversations get an emoji tile and say who spoke last, or that Huub is typing or waiting for an answer. In a conversation, Huub's searches and other steps show as small pills that stay after the reply. Reminders it sets and things it remembers get their own card. The composer, voice recorder, new-conversation screen (with starter ideas), question cards, sign-in and settings screens all got their designed look. Also fixes conversation and settings rows that lost their layout, and a crash when a phone is signed out while a conversation is open.
