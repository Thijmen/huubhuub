HuubHuub moves out of Telegram and gets an app of its own: a friendly chat app for non-technical people, with lots of separate conversations. Below are three brand directions with palette, launcher icon and a Huub mascot, followed by full mockups of the recommended direction and the tokens to build it.

3 · Why Zondag, and the tokens

Why this one

  • It sounds like Huub. Warm, down-to-earth and a little cheeky, the same tone as the persona. Orange plus Delft blue says "Dutch" without flags, tulips or windmills.
  • Orange as seasoning, not sauce. Large areas stay cream, white and ink, so long markdown replies read calmly. Orange marks only Huub and actions (FAB, send, list dots, the scan frame).
  • The mark does a lot of jobs. A speech bubble with a face works as the avatar, typing indicator, empty state, notification icon and Android 13 themed icon. The two-bubble launcher icon literally spells out HuubHuub.
  • It stands out from other AI apps. Most of them are blue, green or purple. Orange is easy to find among her other apps.
  • Dark mode stays cosy. Warm espresso tones instead of blue-grey, which is easier on the eyes in bed at night.

Why not B (Gracht): lovely and calm, but it reads like a bank or utility, and serif titles are less playful. Why not C (Hagelslag): purple is the default AI colour, sprinkles turn to noise at 32 px, and it's louder than "not a clown".
